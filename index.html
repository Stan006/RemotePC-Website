Three steps to total control

No accounts. No cloud. Just direct local connectivity.

1

Install the Windows Listener

Download and run the lightweight listener app on your PC. It remains dormant until you connect, listening only on your secure local network.

2

Pair Your Devices

Launch the Android app and point it to your PC's local IP address. The connection happens instantly over your Wi-Fi.

3

Execute & Transfer

Enjoy low latency for mouse control and high-speed file transfers. Your data never leaves your home Wi-Fi.
