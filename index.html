Before you download

Windows may display a "Windows protected your PC" SmartScreen warning when you run this installer. This is expected as the app is not code signed.

Code signing certificates cost up to $350 per year. For a free app with no monetisation, that overhead simply isn't viable, so we skip it and keep RemotePC free for everyone.

You can read more about this Here — you're welcome to review or build it yourself. To proceed past the warning, click More info → Run anyway.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, security, and setup

The app only requests the minimum permissions required to function—nothing more.

Network access: Used to securely connect to your PC over your local Wi-Fi network and transmit commands in real time. No data is sent to external servers.

Storage access: Only used when you explicitly choose to transfer files. The app never scans, indexes, or accesses your files without your action.

You stay fully in control of what the app can access at all times.

This is a common false positive triggered by how remote-control software works.

Apps like RemotePC simulate keyboard and mouse input, capture the screen, and communicate over the network—behaviors that can resemble malware to automated security systems.

Key differences:

  • No background activity—it only runs when you launch it
  • No telemetry or hidden data collection
  • No cloud communication—everything stays on your local network

Learn more about why this happens: Read explanation

No, access is limited by design.

Only devices connected to your local network (Wi-Fi/LAN) can attempt to connect, and they must know your specific IP address and port, plus the desktop app has features to block even local devices from connecting.

By default, the app is not exposed to the internet, meaning external devices cannot reach your PC unless you explicitly configure advanced networking (such as port forwarding).

This local first approach significantly reduces security risks.

No tracking, no accounts, and no hidden analytics.

The app does not collect personal data, usage metrics, or device information. There are no third-party trackers or background services.

All communication happens directly between your devices over your local network and nothing is routed through external servers.

Your privacy is not just a feature, it’s the default.
